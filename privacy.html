Privacy, plainly stated.

This portfolio uses limited analytics to understand which projects are useful and how visitors find the site.

What is measured

Google Analytics records information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device category, referral source, scrolls, downloads, outbound links, and selected portfolio interactions. This site does not intentionally send names, email addresses, passwords, form entries, or other directly identifying content to Analytics.

Why it is measured

The information is used to evaluate site performance, understand which projects receive attention, and improve the portfolio. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Google Analytics

Analytics data is processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

Browser privacy controls, content blockers, and Google’s Analytics opt-out tools may limit collection. Questions about this site can be sent to gregorymstiles@gmail.com.
