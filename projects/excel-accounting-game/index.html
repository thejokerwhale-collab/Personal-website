Purpose

Teach the systems behind fast accounting work.

Close Sheet is designed around the way accountants actually use Excel: tying out schedules, building formulas once, filling them down, separating assumptions from outputs, surfacing exceptions, and moving through workbooks without relying on the mouse.

What it teaches

Twelve close-work scenarios.

01

Formula Systems

SUM, XLOOKUP, absolute references, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, IF, ABS, and ratio formulas.

02

Accounting Application

Cash receipts, bank recs, A/R aging, variances, accruals, prepaids, depreciation, and margins.

03

Keyboard Efficiency

AutoSum, Fill Down, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter checks, Tab movement, F2 edit mode, and name-box jumps.

04

Guided Learning

Each prompt includes why the step matters, the Excel move, and how it applies to real accounting work.

Game loop

Learn by building the workbook.

  1. Select the target cell in an Excel-like worksheet.
  2. Read the guided accounting purpose and Excel move.
  3. Enter the formula or use the shortcut system.
  4. Fill down where the schedule requires repeatable logic.
  5. Check the answer and earn efficiency points for better workflow.

Open the workbook and start the close.
