Graduate accounting capstone

Polaris stock valuation.

A financial analysis and valuation project evaluating Polaris’ strategy, accounting policies, ratio trends, risk drivers, and future trading price using residual income and free-cash-flow valuation logic.

Ticker: PII Market price analyzed: $64–$67 Intrinsic value: about $30/share Recommendation: Sell

Investment thesis

The market price looked too optimistic.

The capstone report concluded that Polaris appeared significantly overvalued. Conservative assumptions produced an intrinsic value near $30 per share, while the observed market price sat around $64–$67. The gap was driven by a narrow spread between expected return on equity and the cost of equity, weaker profitability, elevated inventory pressure, and consumer demand shifting toward lower-cost powersports options.

Report-backed insights

What drove the valuation call.

01

Strategy Pressure

Polaris relies on differentiation through innovation, premium vehicles, and brand strength, but 2024 demand favored more affordable powersports alternatives.

02

Profitability Decline

ROE fell from 38% in 2022 to 8% in 2024, with DuPont analysis pointing to margin compression rather than leverage as the main driver.

03

Accounting Signals

Conservative R&D expensing, revenue recognition at dealer transfer, inventory build-up, restructuring charges, and adjusted EPS disclosures shaped the financial analysis.

04

Valuation Gap

Residual income and free-cash-flow scenarios both landed far below market price, supporting a sell recommendation under the project’s assumptions.

Valuation model

Residual income as the primary lens.

Cost of equity 10.88%

CAPM using a 4.1% risk-free rate, 1.13 beta, and 6% market risk premium.

ROE forecast 13%

Stable five-year forecast reflecting mature-firm performance and conservative assumptions.

Long-term growth 3%

Continuing-value assumption aligned with mature U.S. GDP-style growth expectations.

Decision Sell

Market valuation required stronger long-run profitability than the analysis supported.
